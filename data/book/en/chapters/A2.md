# Appendix　References and Further Reading

This appendix collects academic literature and continuously maintained databases that can strengthen the arguments of this book. Sources for external events cited in each chapter are listed in that chapter's "Sources for external events in this chapter"; this project's cases and reports are listed in each chapter's "Data sources." The bibliography was checked against the list on Jonathan McDowell's personal publications page (planet4589.org/jcm/pubs, accessed 2026-11-15); for entries marked "Abstract read," the summary follows the paper's abstract, while for the others the relevance is described from the title only, and the original should be consulted before it is cited in the main text.

Citation format: APA 7th edition (author, year, title, publication or website, volume and issue, DOI or URL). Continuously updated databases must state the access date and version.

## 1. Mega-constellations and the low-Earth-orbit population

- McDowell, J. C. (2020). The Low Earth Orbit Satellite Population and Impacts of the SpaceX Starlink Constellation. *The Astrophysical Journal Letters*, *892*, L36. arXiv:2003.07446 Abstract read: reviews the low-Earth-orbit satellite population at the time, points out that Starlink, planned at about 12,000 satellites, will dominate the low-Earth-orbit region below 600 km, and estimates the number of illuminated satellites visible at different latitudes, seasons, and times of day, discussing the impact on ground-based astronomical observation. The abstract does not discuss collision risk or space traffic management. Related chapters: Chapters 3 and 13 (scale of the low-Earth-orbit population and constellation structure).
- Walker, C., Hall, J., et al. (2020). *Impact of Satellite Constellations on Optical Astronomy and Recommendations Towards Mitigations（SATCON1 Report）*. NOIRLab／AAS. McDowell was a participant. Related chapter: Chapter 3 (further reading).

## 2. Space environment, registration, and governance

- Lawrence, A., Rawls, M. L., Jah, M., Boley, A., Di Vruno, F., Garrington, S., Kramer, M., Lawler, S., Lowenthal, J., McDowell, J., & McCaughrean, M. (2022). The case for space environmentalism. *Nature Astronomy*, *6*, 428. doi:10.1038/s41550-022-01655-6 arXiv:2204.10025 Abstract read: argues that orbital space from the Kármán line to geosynchronous orbit should be treated as an environment requiring protection and regulation; the problem is most urgent in low Earth orbit, driven mainly by growth in satellite numbers, including mega-constellations; the harms span professional astronomy, public stargazing, the cultural value of the sky, and the sustainability of commercial, civil, and military space activities. Related chapters: Chapters 1 and 19 (space security has both technical and governance dimensions).
- Jakhu, R. S., Jasani, B., & McDowell, J. C. (2018). Critical issues related to registration of space objects and transparency of space activities. *Acta Astronautica*, *143*, 406. From the title: registration of space objects and transparency of space activities. Related chapters: Chapter 1 (information sharing), Chapter 19 (evidence and transparency needed for decisions).
- Boley, A., et al. (2023). *Montreal Recommendations on Aviation Safety and Uncontrolled Space Object Reentries*. Outer Space Institute. From the title: policy recommendations on uncontrolled space object reentries and aviation safety. Related chapter: Chapter 12 (governance context of the reentry gate).
- McDowell, J., Dalal, S., & Lawler, S. (2025). Responding to New Space Challenges: AAS Statements on Reentry Effects and Spaceflight Transparency. *Bulletin of the AAS*, *57*(2), 238.04. Related chapters: Chapters 12 and 19 (further reading).

## 5. Continuously maintained databases

- McDowell, J. C. (2020–present). *General Catalog of Artificial Space Objects（GCAT）* [Data set]. https://planet4589.org/space/gcat/ A comprehensive catalog of artificial space objects that can serve as an auxiliary source for checking object identity, launch batches, and orbital populations. It is a data source; citations must state the access date and version. Related chapters: Chapter 6 (cataloging and the data population), Chapter 17 (Case 19 catalog number format).
- McDowell, J. C. (1989–present). *Jonathan's Space Report*. https://planet4589.org/space/jsr/jsr.html Issue-by-issue compilation of launch records and preliminary orbits. As of 2026-11-15, the latest issue is No. 861 (2026-11-01), which does not yet include Starship's 15th test flight described in the sidebar of Chapter 3; once it is included, that issue should be cited instead. Related chapter: Chapter 3.

## Principles of use

- Journal papers take precedence over conference abstracts and websites; GCAT and JSR are data sources used to check facts, not sources for arguments.
- Orbital values in launch reports and databases are mostly preliminary; when analyzing orbit raising, station-keeping, or deorbiting, TLE time series, precise ephemerides, or operator data still take precedence.
- This appendix does not cover methodological literature on orbital mechanics, statistical detection, and machine learning; for the methodological basis, see each chapter's "Data sources" and the technical appendix of this project's final report.
