## 中文貼文內容

同一組軌道，兩種說法。

2021 年 7 月 1 日與 10 月 21 日，中國太空站兩度執行「預防碰撞控制」，中方說是 Starlink 衛星逼近。12 月，中國向聯合國秘書長提交照會，指這兩次接近危及航天員生命與健康；2022 年 1 月，美國回覆：美國太空司令部在兩次事件中，都沒有評估出顯著的碰撞機率。

讀者也許想先知道誰對。我在新書第 1 章先問另一個問題：危險出現的當下，誰手上有什麼資料？又能透過什麼管道，及時讓另一方知道？

📘《從衛星軌道論國家安全——太空態勢感知的基礎：智慧化低軌通訊衛星軌道異常與太空事件偵測》
第 1 章　太空已成為國家安全疆域

這一章談三件事：

1️⃣ 衛星出事，影響落在地面
2022 年 Viasat KA-SAT 遭網路攻擊，德國 Enercon 約 5,800 座風機的遠端監控隨之中斷；2024 年 Finnair 因 GPS 干擾，暫停飛往愛沙尼亞塔爾圖的航線一個月。這兩起事件中衛星軌道都沒有改變，所以太空態勢感知不能只看軌道位置。

2️⃣ 資訊共享是分層的
從公開的 NOTSO 通知、JCO 商業資料整合，到會員制的 Space ISAC，再到邀請制、可含機密的 CIC，各自服務不同的信任層級與資訊敏感度。（這部分依 Elara Nova Podcast 整理，尚未獨立查證。）

3️⃣ 公開 TLE 的定位
公開軌道資料精度有限，但覆蓋廣、更新頻繁、可近用，適合當第一層篩選與早期警示，不能取代精密定軌或機密情資。

美國國防情報局 2026 年 10 月的報告評估，中國 ISR 衛星已超過 510 個系統。太空態勢感知是雙向的：我們用公開軌道資料看別人，別人也用自己的監視網看我們。

## English post

One set of orbits, two stories.

On July 1 and October 21, 2021, China's space station performed two collision-avoidance maneuvers. China said Starlink satellites were approaching. That December, China filed a note with the UN Secretary-General saying the close approaches had endangered its astronauts. In January 2022, the US replied that US Space Command had not assessed a significant collision probability in either case.

It is tempting to ask who was right. Chapter 1 of my new book asks a different question first: when danger appears, who holds which data, and through which channel can it reach the other side in time?

📘 National Security from Satellite Orbits
Foundations of Space Situational Awareness: Intelligent Detection of Orbital Anomalies and Space Events for LEO Communication Satellites
Chapter 1: Space Has Become a National Security Domain

Three takeaways:

1️⃣ When satellites fail, the ground feels it
In 2022, a cyberattack on Viasat's KA-SAT network cut remote monitoring of about 5,800 Enercon wind turbines in Germany. In 2024, Finnair suspended its route to Tartu, Estonia, for a month after GPS interference forced two flights to turn back. No orbit changed in either case, so space situational awareness cannot stop at orbital positions.

2️⃣ Information sharing comes in layers
Public NOTSO notices and JCO commercial data fusion, member-based Space ISAC, and the invitation-only, possibly classified CIC each serve a different level of trust and sensitivity. (This part draws on an Elara Nova podcast episode and has not been independently verified.)

3️⃣ Where public TLEs fit
Public orbital data has limited accuracy, but wide coverage, frequent updates and open access. It works as a first-layer screen and early warning. It does not replace precise orbit determination or classified intelligence.

A US Defense Intelligence Agency report from October 2026 assesses that China fields more than 510 ISR satellite systems. Space situational awareness runs both ways: we watch others with public orbital data, and others watch us with their own surveillance networks.
