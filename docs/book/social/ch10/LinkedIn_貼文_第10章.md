## 中文貼文內容

2016 年 3 月，發射才五週多的日本 X 光天文衛星「瞳」（Hitomi）完成一次姿態機動。依 JAXA 事後推定，在地面看不到的弧段裡，衛星對自身旋轉的估計值停在錯誤的高值，系統「認為」衛星正在旋轉，實際上並沒有。反作用輪去抵消這個不存在的旋轉，反而讓衛星真的轉了起來，最後太陽能板等部件斷裂分離。

一個沒有被獨立核對的估計值，被系統當成了事實。當模型對自己算出的數字深信不疑，誰來檢查它？

第 10 章　融合評分器：五個證人，一個信心分數

1️⃣ 五個證人，不是五倍證據
融合評分器的輸入是 CUSUM、BOCPD、奇異譜分析、3σ-MAD 四個統計通道，加上 NRLMSIS 阻力殘差，各取 ±24 小時窗內的統計量，以 284 顆 Starlink 的精密星曆真值訓練。五個通道若依賴同一組 TLE、同一個時間窗，證據的獨立性就會下降。在正式報表的流程中，它不產生候選事件，只影響證據等級。

2️⃣ 泛化不是一個分數
56 顆從未參與訓練的 Starlink：AUC 0.980，大型事件召回 81／81。換成「前段時間訓練、後段時間測試」：AUC 0.945，大型事件召回 0.783。若負樣本只取有真值涵蓋的期間：AUC 0.814。換衛星、換時間、換口徑，回答的是不同問題；部署時須定期重新訓練。

3️⃣ 深度模型輸了，也要寫下來
雙向 GRU 逐時間點評估的天花板 AUC 只有 0.572，原因不在模型能力，而在真值每 8 小時一格，與 TLE 曆元對不齊。結案報告的檢討中，LSTM 自編碼器 AUC 0.704、PatchTST 0.676，都低於融合評分器的 0.982，沒有納入交付系統。

4️⃣ 內部高分，可能是標籤洩漏
Case 23 的分類器用規則自動產生的標籤訓練，修正洩漏後內部召回 97.5%；改用獨立的精密星曆真值驗證，召回降到 39.7%。契約驗收的這一項（事件級召回 0.383）未達標準，經委託單位同意，改以融合評分器（0.973）作為系統層替代指標結案；驗證的是融合評分器，不能說成原模型達標。

0.9 的分數只表示值得優先覆核，不等於 90% 的機率；融合分數再高，系統指出的也只是偏離常態，不等於機動已確認，更不等於敵意或違規。

## English post

In March 2016, Japan's X-ray astronomy satellite Hitomi, barely five weeks after launch, completed an attitude maneuver. According to JAXA's later reconstruction, during a pass out of ground contact the satellite's estimate of its own rotation stuck at a wrong, high value. The system "believed" the satellite was spinning when it was not. The reaction wheels worked against this nonexistent rotation and set the satellite actually spinning; in the end the solar panels and other parts broke off.

An estimate that no one checked independently was treated by the system as fact. When a model fully trusts the number it computed, who checks it?

Foundations of Space Situational Awareness: Intelligent Detection of Orbital Anomalies and Space Events for LEO Communication Satellites
Chapter 10: The Fusion Scorer: Five Witnesses, One Confidence Score

1️⃣ Five witnesses, not five times the evidence
The fusion scorer takes four statistical channels (CUSUM, BOCPD, singular spectrum analysis, 3σ-MAD) plus the NRLMSIS drag residual, each summarized over a ±24-hour window, and is trained on precise-ephemeris ground truth for 284 Starlink satellites. If the channels rely on the same TLEs and the same window, the evidence is less independent than it looks. In the formal report pipeline it creates no candidate events; it only affects the evidence grade.

2️⃣ Generalization is not one number
On 56 Starlink satellites never used in training: AUC 0.980, large-event recall 81/81. Trained on earlier data and tested on later data: AUC 0.945, large-event recall 0.783. With negatives limited to periods covered by ground truth: AUC 0.814. A new satellite, a new time period, and a new evaluation basis answer different questions; deployment needs periodic retraining.

3️⃣ When deep models lose, write it down
A bidirectional GRU evaluated point by point topped out at AUC 0.572, not for lack of model capacity but because the ground truth comes in 8-hour steps that do not line up with TLE epochs. In the final report's review, an LSTM autoencoder reached AUC 0.704 and PatchTST 0.676, both below the fusion scorer's 0.982; neither went into the delivered system.

4️⃣ A high internal score may be label leakage
Case 23's classifier was trained on labels generated by the rules themselves; after a leak was fixed, internal recall was 97.5%. Validated against independent precise-ephemeris truth, recall fell to 39.7%. That contract acceptance item (event-level recall 0.383) did not meet its target; with the client's agreement it was closed using the fusion scorer (0.973) as a system-level substitute. That validates the fusion scorer, not the original model.

A score of 0.9 means "review this first," not a 90% probability. However high the fusion score, the system is pointing to a departure from normal. That is not a confirmed maneuver, let alone evidence of hostility or a violation.
