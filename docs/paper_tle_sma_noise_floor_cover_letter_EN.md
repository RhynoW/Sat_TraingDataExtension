We submit the manuscript "TLE-Derived Semi-Major-Axis Variability on Operator-Logged Maneuver-Free Arcs: Residual Dependence and Empirical Maneuver Thresholds, with Application to FORMOSAT" for consideration as a research article in *Acta Astronautica*.

Maneuver screening from public Two-Line Elements (TLEs) thresholds the change in the TLE-derived semi-major axis, yet the variability behind such thresholds is rarely measured on intervals known to be free of maneuvers, and thresholds are usually set as a multiple of a single-sample scale without checking the distribution of the actual decision statistic. Using maneuver-free arcs defined by the operator logs of thirteen International DORIS Service altimetry satellites, the manuscript reports:

1. An arc-scale variability of 0.23–2.08 m (median 0.75 m) at 719–1338 km, and strong persistence of adjacent detrended residuals (median lag-one Spearman correlation 0.81), which makes adjacent-difference scales an increment measure rather than a single-element-set error.
2. A calibration of the step statistic on 61,923 pseudo-event windows: a nominal 3σ threshold gives false-alarm probabilities of 3–96% per window, and in-sample empirical thresholds of 2–57 m are needed; random arc-grouped validation gives near-nominal exceedance for twelve satellites, while chronological validation reveals nonstationarity for three; for Jason-3 a 2.0 m threshold flags 67 of 68 logged SMA maneuvers in known windows.
3. A test against operator three-component Δv showing satellite-dependent agreement of the along-track component on clean brackets (pooled r = 0.942), and an application to the FORMOSAT series that separates candidate maneuvers from apparent constellation-wide differences that span multi-day gaps in the retrieved TLE series and are, in three of four clusters, mostly accumulated decay.

The results provide a measured, statistic-specific basis for calibrating maneuver-screening thresholds in space situational awareness.

The manuscript is original, has not been published, and is not under consideration elsewhere. A companion manuscript on the detection sensitivity of TLE-based maneuver detection, calibrated against FORMOSAT-7 precise orbits, has been submitted to *Advances in Space Research* (reference 14); the two papers do not overlap in data analysis.
